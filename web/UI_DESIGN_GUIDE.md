# AlphaMatrix Backtesting App - UI Design Guide

_Hey friend! 👋 This is a simple guide to what our trading strategy testing app should look like and do. Think of it like a video game where you can test different strategies before using real money!_

## What is Backtesting? 🎯

Imagine you have an idea for a trading strategy - like "buy Apple stock when it goes up 5% in a day, then sell it the next day." Backtesting lets you test this idea using historical data to see if it would have made money in the past. It's like having a time machine to test your trading ideas!

## The Main App Interface 🖥️

### 1. **The Dashboard (Home Screen)**

- **Think of it like:** Your phone's home screen, but for trading strategies
- **What you'll see:**
  - Quick overview of how your strategies are performing
  - Recent test results
  - Alerts and notifications
  - Quick access to your favorite strategies

### 2. **Strategy Builder (The "Recipe Maker")**

- **Think of it like:** Building a recipe in a cooking app
- **What you'll do:**
  - Drag and drop different trading rules (like ingredients)
  - Set conditions like "buy when price is low, sell when price is high"
  - Choose which stocks to trade
  - Set how much money to risk on each trade
- **Visual tools:**
  - Simple sliders and buttons (no complex math needed!)
  - Preview of what your strategy looks like
  - Templates to get started quickly

### 3. **Backtesting (The "Time Machine")**

- **Think of it like:** Playing a video game with historical data
- **What happens:**
  - Pick a date range (like "test this strategy from 2020 to 2023")
  - Watch the app simulate your strategy day by day
  - See real-time results as the simulation runs
  - Get a final report of how well it performed

### 4. **Results & Charts (The "Report Card")**

- **Think of it like:** A detailed report card for your strategy
- **What you'll see:**
  - **Profit/Loss Chart:** A line showing if you made or lost money over time
  - **Key Numbers:**
    - Total money made/lost
    - How risky the strategy was
    - How often it won vs. lost
    - Worst losing streak
  - **Trade List:** Every single trade the strategy made
  - **Risk Analysis:** How much money you could lose in bad scenarios

## Key Features That Make It User-Friendly 🎨

### **Visual Design**

- **Dark theme:** Easy on the eyes (like your phone's dark mode)
- **Big buttons:** Easy to click and navigate
- **Color coding:** Green for profits, red for losses, yellow for warnings
<!-- - **Simple language:** No confusing financial jargon -->

### **Smart Defaults**

- **Templates:** Pre-built strategies to get started quickly
- **Wizards:** Step-by-step guides for building strategies
- **Auto-save:** Never lose your work
- **Undo/Redo:** Easy to fix mistakes

### **Real-Time Updates**

- **Live progress:** See your test running in real-time
- **Instant feedback:** Know immediately if something's wrong
- **Notifications:** Get alerts for important events
- **Auto-refresh:** Data updates automatically

## The User Experience Flow 🔄

5. **Learn and repeat** → Build confidence gradually

1. **Build custom strategies** → Create from scratch
2. **Advanced testing** → Use multiple time periods
